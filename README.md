## Project Overview

Zuri Electronics needed visibility into their sales performance across customer segments, product categories, and geographic regions. Leadership had raw data exports but no centralized reporting.

### Objective
- Clean and transform raw data exports into an analysis-ready dataset
- Build a star schema data model with DAX measures
- Deliver an interactive executive dashboard for stakeholders

### Tools Used
- **Power BI** — Dashboard design and visualization
- **DAX** — 12+ calculated measures (revenue, profit margins, YoY trends)
- **Power Query** — Data cleaning and transformation
- **Star Schema** — Data modeling

## Key Findings

| Insight | Detail |
|---------|--------|
| Corporate Segment Gap | Underperforming at 27% of revenue ($1.17M) — $500K behind Consumer, signaling untapped B2B potential |
| Seasonal Revenue Dip | Recurring June-August decline (~25%) presents opportunity for seasonal promotions |
| Product Profitability | Technology products yield 2-3x the profit of Furniture, yet the West region underindexes on Tech sales |

## Dashboard Preview

The dashboard includes:
- KPI cards (Total Revenue, Total Profit, Orders, Average Order Value)
- Revenue by Country (bar chart)
- Revenue by Product Category (bar chart)
- Revenue by Employee (bar chart)
- Shipper Revenue distribution (donut chart)
- Revenue by Year trend (line chart)
- Freight Analysis (pie chart)
- Interactive slicers for date, category, and country filtering
